# Varynx Day 61 — Unified Behavioral Intelligence Pipeline

## 1. Overview

Day 61 introduces the **Unified Behavioral Intelligence Pipeline** for Varynx.

The purpose of this component is to provide a single orchestration layer connecting the behavioral intelligence, counterfactual security estimation, predictive analysis, and runtime decision components developed during Days 46–60.

Day 61 does not introduce another independent risk-scoring algorithm.

Instead, it coordinates the outputs of existing Varynx components into a structured end-to-end security-intelligence pipeline.
